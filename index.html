Why this server?

Built for the player who logs in for an hour, makes real progress, and logs off without feeling behind.

Less grind, vanilla difficulty

1.5x wood and stone, 1.25x ores, 1.75x stacks. Gather rates that respect your time, but you'll still die fairly to a better player.

Long days, short nights

55-minute days, 5-minute nights. Less time stumbling in the dark, more time playing.

Decay is forgiving

50% decay scale means your base survives the days you can't log in. Normal upkeep brackets - just maintain your TC.

Bi-weekly wipes

Every 1st and 3rd Thursday at 3 PM EST. Map wipes always; blueprints wipe with Facepunch's monthly forced wipe (1st Thursday).

Max team 4 - strictly enforced

No mega-clans. Solo, duo, trio, or quad. Everyone has a fair shot.

Hidden raidable bases

Random NPC-free raidable bases scattered across the map at all times. No domes, no markers - if you find one, it's yours to take.

Find us in the server browser

Look for this banner under the Modded tab. "Show empty servers" filter must be on while we're seeding.

House rules

Be cool, have fun, go to bed on time.

Don't

  • Cheat, hack, or script (instant ban)
  • Use racism, hate speech, or targeted harassment
  • Exploit game bugs or glitches intentionally
  • Door camp for extended periods (5+ minutes)
  • Spam chat or voice
  • Coordinate with other teams to bypass the team limit

Do

  • Have fun. It's a game.
  • Be respectful in chat
  • Raid and PvP freely - all fair game
  • Report cheaters to an admin
  • Build cool bases - we might add yours to the rotation
  • Touch grass occasionally

Breaking rules: warning → kick → ban. Cheating: instant permanent ban.
